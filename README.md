# Manga Tracker ES

A web-based platform tailored for manga enthusiasts in Spain to seamlessly track their reading progress and manage their personal collections.

## 🚀 Features

*   **Reading Progress Tracker:** Keep tabs on your current volume and chapter for every series you are reading.
*   **Collection Management:** Catalog your physical or digital manga volumes with a clean, intuitive interface.
*   **Spanish Market Integration:** Designed specifically for manga readers in Spain, accounting for local editions and publishing houses (Panini, Ivrea, Milky Way, etc.).
*   **Personal Statistics:** Visual overviews of your reading habits and collection growth.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Frontend:** Angular 21
*   **Backend:** Java 25
*   **Database:** PosgreSQL
